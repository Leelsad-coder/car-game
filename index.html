Master the art of car maintenance. Keep it fast. Keep it clean. Keep it alive.

Essential Maintenance

01. Engine Oil

Change oil every 5,000 - 8,000 km. Clean oil = longer engine life + better fuel economy. Check dipstick monthly.

02. Tire Pressure

Keep tires at recommended PSI. Proper pressure improves grip, fuel efficiency, and prevents blowouts.

03. Coolant & Fluids

Flush coolant every 2 years. Check brake fluid, power steering, and windshield washer regularly.

04. Battery Care

Clean terminals, check voltage. A healthy battery prevents breakdowns. Replace every 3-4 years.

05. Brakes & Suspension

Listen for squeaks. Replace brake pads before rotors get damaged. Get alignment done every 10,000 km.

06. Car Wash & Wax

Wash weekly. Wax every 3 months. Protects paint from UV and rust. A clean car runs cooler.

Maintenance Schedule
